# Changelog

## 0.4.0

- Rebuilt. Eight one-click styles (Punch, Tilted, Boxed, Glow, Comic, Clean, Marker, Shout), each previewed on your own frame.
- Six thumbnail fonts bundled (Anton, Bebas Neue, Archivo Black, Bangers, Luckiest Guy, Permanent Marker), plus Impact.
- Highlight words with `*stars*`. Outline, shadow, glow or boxed text; tilt, width and spacing.
- Drag the title, badge and stickers on the canvas, and drag the picture to move it; scroll to resize. Snaps to the middle.
- An arrow and a circle to point at things. A shade behind the text, blur, brightness and contrast for the background.
- A preview at YouTube's sizes (home, search, up next; dark or light), and a guide for where the video's length covers the corner.
- Drop an image on the canvas. Save as PNG, or JPG under YouTube's 2 MB limit.
- A colour icon.

## 0.3.0

- With no project open, **Save** puts the PNG in **Downloads › AnyCut › Thumbnail Maker** (asks for `downloads`).
- A cover for its card in the Extensions view.

## 0.2.0

- The controls moved into the tab's properties panel, so the canvas gets the whole main area.
- The tab shows its sequence's Timeline below. With **Follow the playhead** on, scrubbing it changes the background frame.

## 0.1.0

- First version: a frame of the timeline (or any image), a big title with outline, a badge, darken, saturation, zoom and vignette, saved as a 1280×720 PNG into the project.
